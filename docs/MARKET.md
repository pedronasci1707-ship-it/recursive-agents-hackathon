# Raleigh resale price check — October 17, 2026

CampusLoop's pilot targets move-out and move-in near NC State. Prices below are **demo asking prices**, not measured average sale prices. The public evidence is sparse and mixes different sizes, brands and conditions. We checked local seller listings and current retail comparables, then set conservative category defaults. A real operation should compare the exact model, age, tested operation and recent completed local sales before paying for stock.

The demo's catalog contains illustrative products and [attributed Wikimedia photos](../static/CREDITS.md), not evidence that those exact physical units were inspected.

## Pricing defaults used in the app

The resale column is generated by `campusloop/rules.py`: category A base × quality factor. B is normally 75% and C 50% of A. When the vision agent supplies a 1–10 score, the factor ranges from 40% (score 3) to 100% (score 10). Scores 1–2 are blocked for human review. Offers to sellers target 35% of the resale price; this is **our business assumption**, not a measured local buyback rate. Acquisition, refurbishment and $20 kit delivery must still leave at least a 30% kit margin.

The photo gives a visible-condition estimate; cooling, electrical safety and exact model require inspection at pickup. Recalled models are blocked by the demo recall list before entering inventory.

## Validation still needed before a real launch

Record recent **completed** local transactions for each exact model and size, test price acceptance with incoming students, and measure pickup, cleaning, storage, return and delivery costs. Update the defaults per campus and season. The demo prices should not be treated as appraisals of individual goods.
